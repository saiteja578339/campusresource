Which CNN is used?

MobileNetV2 is loaded through TensorFlow/Keras with ImageNet weights. Images are resized to 224×224 pixels and passed through the network to identify likely objects. It is lightweight enough for a student project and can later be fine-tuned with campus-item images.

  • Pre-trained on ImageNet
  • Depthwise separable convolutions
  • Fast inference on normal laptops
  • Pillow quality fallback without TensorFlow

Price prediction

Random Forest regression learns nonlinear relationships between category, condition, demand and photo quality. Comparable campus listings are blended into the result.

Recommendation

TF-IDF converts listing text and student interests into vectors. Cosine similarity is blended with freshness, trust, demand and affordability.

Fraud detection

Isolation Forest finds unusual prices while explainable rules detect duplicates, risky language and severe deviation from the fair-price range.
